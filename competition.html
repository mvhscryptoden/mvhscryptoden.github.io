What are Crypto Den competitions?

Crypto Den competitions take place throughout the semester and are free and open to all students. During these competitons (hosted on HTMW) students can test their skills and compete against each other. The winner is the student with the highest % returned. The winner recieves a 25$ in-n-out gift card!
