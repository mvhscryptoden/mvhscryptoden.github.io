What are Crypto Den competitions?

Crypto Den competitions take place throughout the semester and are free and open to all students. Hosted on HowTheMarketWorks, competitions let students test their investing skills and compete against one another. The winner is the student with the highest percentage return and receives a $15 In-N-Out gift card.

How it works:

1. Join the Competition

Use the button above to access the current CryptoDen competition.

2. Build your portfolio

Make your investment decisions using the available virtual funds.

3. Watch the market and make trades

Track your portfolio and adjust your strategy as the market changes.

4. See how you rank

Compare your performance with other CryptoDen members.
